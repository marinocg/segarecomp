# ADR 0202: SEG-092-T001 - compilation-cost attribution of A+B35 versus M3+B35 (Checkpoint C1): `PLAUSIBLE_COST_CAUSES_IDENTIFIED`

- Status: Accepted (research, SEG-092-T001, Checkpoint C1 only). Follows ADR 0157 (cost baselines), the model comparison ADRs 0165/0169/0170 and SEG-091's incumbent-preserving comparison.
  No ADR is amended. **No product change, no default change, no selection/prune/closure/validator/runtime change, no new budget, no retraining.** M3+B35 (`--immutable-rom-aot-ml-gen2-admission`) is unchanged and remains the incumbent.
- Privacy: aggregate only (ratios, counts, byte totals). ROM bytes, addresses, generated C, per-address and per-translation-unit data stay in the private harness; the pre-registration, tooling and per-image results are harness-private.
- Scope: attribution only. The mechanisms below are **candidates from paired measurements**; causality is the job of SEG-092-T002. Nothing here qualifies model A for deployment (it has no native implementation).

## Question

SEG-091 measured model A at the same B35 budget about 9 % cheaper in total cold-build CPU than the deployed M3+B35 route over eleven development images, without an explanation and under a non-randomised order with concurrent workers. Why?

## Method (pre-registered before measurement)

Product `0a530ba`, clang 18.1.3 `-O2 -std=c11 -Wall -Wextra -Werror -pedantic`, 4 compile jobs, no object cache, cold; SEG-091's exact selections and score snapshots; unchanged prune/closure/validator.
Nine images (seven lineages): four expected large gains (Sonic 1, Sonic 2, Sonic & Knuckles, Shining Force II), one weak (Shining Force), one borderline (Phantasy Star II), three controls (Flicky, two SGDK).
Arms INC, A35_final, A35_oof (compiled) and an emit-only M3-through-plan arm whose generated C was byte-identical to INC's on every image and repetition. Three cold repetitions (Shining Force: two), arm order randomised per image/repetition, one serial worker on an idle-checked host,
per-translation-unit CPU from `wait4`. 13,430 CPU s spent of a 24,000 ceiling; no abort, failure or re-run. Generated C was digest-identical across repetitions for every arm.

## Results

1. **The advantage reproduces under randomised order on an idle serial host.** Pooled over the nine images: INC 1,757.9 CPU s, A35_final 1,608.4 (-8.5 %), A35_oof 1,640.0 (-6.7 %). A35_final/INC per image: Sonic 1 0.896, Sonic 2 0.870, Sonic & Knuckles 0.904,
   Shining Force II 0.918, Shining Force 0.920, Phantasy Star II 0.934, SGDK d09 0.978, SGDK d05 0.952 (inside its 5.6 % noise), **Flicky 1.068 (significantly worse)**. Every ratio is within 0.07 of SEG-091's. Lineage-mean 0.957 / worst 1.068 (SEG-091: 0.961 / 1.072),
   i.e. the frozen SEG-091 adoption gate would fail again. INC repetition spread is 0.6-2.3 % (d05 5.6 %). Absolute INC CPU is 9-20 % below SEG-091's (it ran concurrent workers).
2. **Equal admitted-identity counts do not mean equal cost.** The admitted identity totals differ by only 0.1-1.2 % between arms, but entry segments fall 3-10 % and generated C 1-9 % on the gaining images
   (Sonic 2: 44,246 -> 39,647 entry segments, 120.5 -> 109.2 MB; Shining Force II: 87,633 -> 80,207, 254.8 -> 234.5 MB). The reverse happens on Flicky (7,147 -> 7,609 segments, +6.7 % bytes).
3. **Decomposition of the claimed A35_final delta (-150.2 CPU s over the seven images with a claimed gain):** generated-C volume of the `aot`, `shared` and `block` translation-unit families 74 % (56 % `aot`, 11 % `shared`, 7 % `block`);
   per-byte compile intensity 16 % (`aot` about 0.4-3.8 % cheaper per byte on 8 of 9 images; `block` on one image with two repetitions, weak); inference + plan + emit stage 9 % (native M3 inference inside INC's emit is 0.2-4.9 s per image);
   link and all other families about 0 %. A35_oof gives the same ranking. Across the nine images log(CPU ratio) correlates 0.976 with log(generated-bytes ratio) (slope 1.16), in both signs.
4. **The difference is set by which identities are exchanged, not by how common ones are emitted.** Between 5 % and 43 % of the admitted identities are exclusive to one arm; the emitted entry-segment bytes of every common identity are identical (0 B difference on eight images, 1.3 kB of 124 MB on one).
   INC's exclusive identities amortise fewer identities per entry segment (Sonic 2: 2.67 vs 3.70; Sonic 1 2.23 vs 2.74; Sonic & Knuckles 3.19 vs 4.71; Shining Force II 3.45 vs 4.17) and cost more generated bytes per identity (Sonic 2: 562 vs 397 B). On Flicky it is reversed (3.78 vs 2.62).
5. **Fragmentation is neither necessary nor sufficient.** Contiguous admitted cell runs fall 17-53 % on five gaining images but are unchanged on Shining Force I (-0.2 %) and nearly so on II (-2.5 %), which still gain 8 %; SGDK d09 halves its runs for a 2 % gain; Flicky's runs rise 9 % with its cost.
6. **Not sources:** link (0.1-0.3 s), translation-unit count (equal), translation-unit size concentration, precise-discovery block function counts (equal on every image), sharing/closure emission of common identities.

## Checkpoint C1 decision

Frozen rule: benefit reproduced in sign on all four large images, and one component (generated-C volume of the exchanged identities, `aot` family) explains >= 25 % of the claimed delta in both A35_final and A35_oof -> **`PLAUSIBLE_COST_CAUSES_IDENTIFIED`**. Ranked candidates for T002:
(H1) generated-C volume per admitted identity of the exchanged cells (about 74 %); (H2) per-byte compile intensity (about 16 %, weak); (H3) native M3 inference inside INC's emit versus cheaper A inference (about 9 %, tied to the model implementation); (H4) fragmentation (weak). 
The property of the exchanged cells that lowers their segment/byte volume (for example branch-target or block-leader density) is **not identified** and is T002's first question; whether a model-free change of M3's B35 composition or of generated-C organisation can obtain it is open.

## Limits and non-claims

Development images only (INC in-sample on five titles); Shining Force I with two repetitions; model-A inference is research Python and has no native implementation, so a deployable A would add inference cost not measured here; identity-level analysis uses entry-segment bytes as the generated-C proxy;
correlation across nine images with one reversed point; verified-start retention and runtime equivalence were not re-measured (SEG-091's stand; nothing here changes selection). No completeness claim, no adoption proposal, no change to M3+B35.
