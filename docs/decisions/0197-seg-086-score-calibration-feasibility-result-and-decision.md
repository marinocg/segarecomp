- Status: Accepted (SEG-086, checkpoints C4/C5). Follows ADRs 0195-0196; none is amended. Protocol, formula choice, calibration rule, margin, abstention conditions, success definitions and compute ceiling are frozen in plan `tools/segarecomp_seg086_cal.plan.json` (digest `d5b2d43d...6e90ba1`, committed before the final evaluation).
- Evidence: `tools/segarecomp_seg086_cal.results.json` (aggregate; two identical runs, sha256 `2957098525e6...`), `tools/segarecomp_seg086_report.json`, `tools/segarecomp_seg086_cal.h01.json`, `tools/segarecomp_seg086_diag.results.json`, `tools/segarecomp_seg086_cal.selection.json`.

## Decision: `CALIBRATION_INEFFECTIVE`

Applying the frozen ladder (ADR 0196) as written: `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` is excluded (isolation gates hold - the RAW control reproduces the SEG-085 isolated `DYN_SCORE` threshold in all eleven folds, two spot refits reproduce the stored inner scores bitwise, two full runs are byte-identical, eight or more lineages evaluable); `RANKING_LIMITATION` is excluded (the per-image oracle normalised
threshold exceeds the 0.60 cap on 0 % of the 40 domain images); `RECALL_FAILED` does not apply (no verified start is missed); gates A, S and D all **fail**: the chosen normaliser (N2) with the frozen margin **falls back to broad AOT on all 40 domain images** (fallback share 1.00 of images and cells; retained 1.0 everywhere; no selected image, so no dynamics).
The economics gate was therefore not reached and no compile benchmark was run (frozen: economics only if R, A, S and D hold). Zero missed starts here is vacuous - a universal fallback retains every cell - and is not a recall result.

The cause is the same mechanism as SEG-085's `DYN_SCORE`, now in the normalised space. The held-out thresholds `tau_H` are between -4.9 and -1.1 normalised units; the plateau sits at about 0 (`z = s - Q10`) and the nearest weakest positive at about +1.5, so **every cell of every domain image passes** (fraction of cells at or above `tau_H`: 1.0 on all 40 images). The margin is the leave-one-lineage-out shortfall of the plain minimum rule among the calibration lineages
(2.8 to 5.2 units per fold; 3.1 for the Sonic fold), which is larger than the entire distance between the data plateau and the weakest verified positive. Normalisation removed the image-level location differences (<= about 1.5 logits) but those were never the dominant term.

## Required report

1. **Why do model-A logits vary so much between lineages?** They do not vary as a scale: the data plateau is stable (per-lineage negative-score median -10.9 to -12.3) and the final-model plateau equals the leave-one-lineage-out one to within 0.25 logits for ten lineages. What varies is the **extreme order statistic of a few cells**: per-lineage weakest-positive scores span 20.7 logits (-10.3 to +10.4), and only 205 of 14,186 non-seed positive cells score below 0, in three lineages
   (D01 132, D12 47, D03 26). Those 205 cells are mostly mixed or data-dominated 128-byte cells containing a few starts (148 mixed, 122 mostly data, 26 single-start, against 4,557 mixed of 13,981 and 29 single-start for the rest). The SEG-085 threshold (-13.66) is below the minimum score of every development image. (ADR 0195.)
2. **Can simple label-free normalisation correct the scale?** Not in a way that transfers. N2 (plateau shift) is invariant to the image-level location but leaves the lineage-minimum spread unchanged (20.7 units); the model-to-model shift of the weakest positive between the 9-lineage and 10-lineage fits is unchanged (median 0.58, maximum 5.5 for D01). N1 (median/IQR) is unusable on plateau-dominated images (two scale failures
   on images of at least 64 cells; its scale tracks unlabeled code-like mass (ADR 0195)) and its margin-free rule misses 15 starts. In-sample, at the full-recall threshold, neither beats the raw control (cell-weighted retained RAW 0.329, N1 0.388, N2 0.342).
3. **Does a normalised threshold produce genuinely dynamic budgets?** The frozen policy produces none: 40 of 40 fallbacks. The *diagnostic* margin-free N2 rule (not the policy) does vary (retained 0.03-1.0, 37 selected images), which shows the normalisation itself can respond to the image; the margin removes it.
4. **Does proper calibration preserve all verified development instruction starts?** Vacuously yes for the frozen policy (everything is retained). Disclosed misses of non-policy variants: the disqualified N1 under the frozen margin missed 1 start (D01 `sk`, in a mixed cell); margin-free rules missed 1 (RAW, D01 `s2`, mixed cell) and 15 (N1; 8 in D01 `SPMM__`, 6 in `sk`, 1 in `s2`, all mixed
   cells). The margin-free N2 diagnostic missed none on 11 nested folds. Mixed code/data cells account for every disclosed miss; dense (all-code) images cannot be selected under the 0.60 cap by definition.
5. **Which images still require broad fallback?** All 40 domain images (36 historical: 32 Sonic images - ROMs and Sonic CD modules - plus Flicky, Phantasy Star II and the two Shining Force images; 4 SGDK: d05, d06, d07, d09), plus 4 all-code Sonic CD modules (cap-infeasible), 3 images below 64 cells (fail closed), and 3 SGDK images without a seed universe (`unsupported`, outside the domain). Normalisation failures on images of at least 64 cells: 0 for N2 (2 for N1). Validator rejections: none (no region was proposed, so nothing reached the closure and validator).
6. **What savings versus B50 and the deployed incremental policy?** None. The frozen policy costs the broad build. The same 40 images: B50 retains 0.50 (0 missed starts), B35 0.35 (8,015 missed starts, 7,837 of them in the four SGDK images, mostly `d07`), unchanged ADAPT 0.43 lineage-balanced / 0.30 cell-weighted (1 missed start), isolation-corrected ADAPT 0.55 / 0.60 (4 fallbacks, 0 missed), `DYN_SCORE` 1.00 (40 fallbacks), frozen N2 1.00, margin-free N2 diagnostic 0.51 / 0.56 (3 fallbacks).
   Measured costs from SEG-085 apply to the fallback behaviour (broad AOT = 1.48 x B50 build CPU on the exposed image) and were not re-measured; no new compile benchmark was run (frozen gate). Inference and normalisation overhead per image is about 1.5 CPU-s plus a sort.
7. **Which results are independent and which reused?** None is independent. Everything is development evidence on eleven lineages that also informed the formula choice and each other's calibration; the SEG-085 inner refits and out-of-fold scores are reused (verified), the final-model scores of the exposed image are reused. The exposed Phantasy Star IV image (H01), scored only after the freeze through the frozen-manifest `EvaluationContext`:
   frozen policy `abstain_cap` (retained 1.0, 0 missed starts); margin-free diagnostic (not the policy; not decision-relevant) selected 0.297 with 0 missed starts. **REUSED_HISTORICAL_DIAGNOSTIC - NOT INDEPENDENT CONFIRMATION**; it counts toward no minimum.
8. **Calibration limitation or ranking problem?** A calibration/margin limitation, with a ranking-granularity component that calibration cannot remove. Ranking inside images is adequate (0 ranking-error images; oracle full-recall fraction at most 0.46; one verified positive in the corpus scores below the 90th percentile of its own image's negatives). The binding term is the weakest-tail mixed cells of three lineages, whose position in other lineages
   cannot be predicted from the calibration lineages, which forces a margin as large as the plateau-to-signal distance.
9. **Should the next milestone acquire new historical evaluation lineages for confirmation?** Not for this policy: it failed its own development gates, so there is nothing to confirm, and the ladder forbids expanding calibration architectures without a new decision. If the operator decides to continue, new untouched lineages would be needed first to *estimate* the tail margin (the 11 development lineages have a 3-5 logit shortfall because one or two lineages
   carry the tail); that is a data question, and a margin-free or small-margin rule examined on the same development data or on H01 is not evidence.
10. **Are additional labels or different models necessary?** Nothing here indicates it. Ranking is adequate and B50 remains the deployed-quality reference. The one specific limiting class is mixed code/data cells at 128-byte granularity; neither retraining model A nor B/C-v2 was tested or is indicated. Targeted labels for mixed cells would be the logical research direction only if a future decision pursues dynamic budgets again.

## Procedure and integrity notes

- Evaluation-only safeguards (ADR 0195): role preservation in restored metadata (harness PR #104), training/calibration refusal, explicit `EvaluationContext`, frozen-manifest membership, reserved identities first; synthetic tests only. No sealed, reserved or SEG-077 material was opened; the ToeJam & Earl exposure remains recorded and ineligible.
- Two amendments to the pre-registered formula-selection rule were recorded **after the first run of the selection command showed the rule disqualified both candidates on a technicality** (ADR 0195); the first-run numbers had been seen. The final choice (N2) follows from the amended rule; the unamended rule would have produced no choice. N1 is reported as a disqualified diagnostic.
- An exploratory nested run preceded the freeze (ADR 0196) and exposed that the shortfall margin leads to universal fallback; the margin rule is the SEG-085 precedent the brief said to reuse and was not changed afterwards. The margin-free rule is reported as a diagnostic only and could not determine the decision.
- Compute: about 3 CPU-minutes of 8 CPU-hours (one final-model refit 60 s, two spot refits 85 s, the rest reuses stored scores); economics 0 of 12 CPU-hours.
- Not a completeness guarantee: a zero-miss result means zero missed *verified* starts on the evaluated data. Boundaries remain the reconstruction authors'.
