This package redistributes the following third-party components unmodified, under their own licenses:

* SDL3 (Simple DirectMedia Layer), zlib license                       -> SDL3.txt
* Zig 0.15.2 toolchain (bundled only to compile generated C), MIT     -> Zig.txt
  The Zig distribution includes LLVM/Clang (Apache-2.0 with LLVM exceptions), compiler-rt, and libc
  headers/sources (musl, mingw-w64, glibc/libc++ stubs and headers) under their own licenses; those texts
  ship inside toolchain/lib next to the files they cover.
* Dear ImGui (launcher user interface), MIT                            -> DearImGui.txt

Source for Zig and SDL3 is available from https://ziglang.org and https://libsdl.org at the pinned versions
listed in packaging/README.md of the Segarecomp source repository.
